Flicksy is **$24.99 once**. There is no subscription and no Flicksy account. The purchase includes all 1.x updates.

## Trial

The direct download includes the complete app. Click **Start 14-Day Free Trial** in Flicksy to begin. The clock does not start at download, does not renew, and never charges you.

When the trial ends, your folders and Flicksy organization stay on disk. Buy a license to keep using the app.

## Buy and activate

1. Buy from [flicksy.me](/#pricing) or **Flicksy → Buy Flicksy…** in the app.
2. Checkout emails a license key.
3. Paste the key into Flicksy and choose **Activate**.

You can recover the key later through [Creem My Orders](https://creem.io/my-orders/login). Keep the key private.

## Three Macs

A license can be active on up to three Macs. To move a seat, open **Flicksy → Flicksy License…** on the Mac you are leaving and choose **Deactivate This Mac**. That requires a network connection. Then activate on the new Mac.

If all three seats are in use, Flicksy will say so when you try to activate another machine.

## Updates

Direct purchases include all 1.x updates. Check for them from **Flicksy → Check for Updates…**
