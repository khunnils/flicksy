Flicksy is a paid-upfront Mac App Store app. There is no subscription and no Flicksy account.

## Trial

The direct download includes the complete app. Click **Start 14-Day Free Trial** in Flicksy to begin. The clock does not start at download, does not renew, and never charges you.

When the trial ends, your folders and Flicksy organization stay on disk. Buy and install Flicksy from the Mac App Store to continue.

## Buy on the App Store

Choose **View on App Store** from the trial screen or **Flicksy → View on App Store**. Buy Flicksy with your Apple Account, then install the App Store version. The App Store version verifies the paid download automatically.

## Updates and refunds

App Store installations receive updates through Apple. For redownloads, use the Purchased list for the Apple Account that bought Flicksy. Apple handles refund requests through [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
