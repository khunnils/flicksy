Flicksy is built so you can judge media before another player window opens.

## Scrub video in the grid

On the Images & Video grid, move across a video thumbnail to skim the clip. The frame under the pointer is the preview. Click the play control on a cell when you want inline playback instead of scrubbing.

## Open a preview

Select one or more items and press **Space**.

In the viewer:

- **←** and **→** move to the previous or next item.
- **Space** plays or pauses video and audio.
- **F** toggles full screen.
- **Esc** closes the viewer.
- **+** and **−** zoom an image.

## Compare images

Select two to four images and press **⇧Space**, or choose compare from the viewer toolbar or command palette.

Flicksy places the images in a layout you can change until the best option is obvious. Press **⇧Space** again to return to a single preview.

## Crop, rotate, and flip

Image preview includes native crop, rotate, and flip. Crop guides include common print aspect ratios.

Applying a crop **overwrites the original image**. There is no separate export. If you need a copy, duplicate the file first from the command palette or context menu.

Rotate and flip also write back to the original file.

For audio inspection and trimming, see [Audio](/docs/audio).
